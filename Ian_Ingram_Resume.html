AI product

Ask Amenti · source-grounded Q&A

  • Search-first by design: names and fragments resolve against local indexes with no model call; only a real question spends money.
  • Every quotation is checked against the text fetched that turn and marked verified, from a librarian's note, or unmatched; a tally prints even on clean answers so readers can see the check ran.
  • Answers only from shared registers (roster, library, events), so a wrong answer exposes a wrong record; this caught and fixed a figure misdated by more than a century.

Architecture

Conversation and voice engine

  • Separated thinking from speaking: Claude composes the in-character reply, Gemini renders the voice, and a Cloudflare Worker routes between them, so either vendor can change without retuning the other.
  • Bounded each conversation to a 14-message window, so a three-hour conversation costs the same per turn as the first.
  • Archived rendered audio instead of caching it briefly, so audio cost scales with distinct content rather than audience size.

Cost analysis

Measured AI unit economics

  • Built and measured the real prompt instead of estimating: $0.0138 per turn, with the system prompt making up 57% of input.
  • Compared levers side by side (lean prompt, shorter window, smaller model, caching) and showed prompt caching saves 22–33% with no loss of quality, breaking even on the second turn.
